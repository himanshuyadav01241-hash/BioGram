Complete Integration Guide

How do I set up the Discord Lanyard Widget?

Join the official Lanyard Discord server, enable Developer Mode in Discord, copy your numerical User ID, and paste it into your Account Hub customizer.

Why won't YouTube music links play audio?

YouTube links are HTML video pages, not raw audio streams. Upload direct MP3 files to Catbox.moe or Discord attachments to get direct .mp3 links that play instantly.
