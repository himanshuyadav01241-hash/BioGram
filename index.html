Frequently Asked Questions

Is BioGram free to use? +

Yes! BioGram is completely free. Customize your bio page, add audio streams, and link social media at zero cost.

How does the Discord live status widget work? +

BioGram integrates with the Lanyard REST API. Simply enter your numeric Discord User ID in the profile settings, and your real-time status updates instantly on your biolink.
