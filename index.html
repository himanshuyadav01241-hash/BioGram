How to Customize Your Space

Connect live music, Lanyard Discord activity, and custom interactive widgets in minutes.

01

Claim Your Unique Handle

Reserve your custom biogram.me/username link using Google authentication. Manage or update your handle anytime inside Account Settings.

02

Background Music & Playlists

Paste any Spotify track or playlist URL. The cover art and link will display on your space while audio plays seamlessly in the background.

03

Sync Discord via Lanyard

Enter your Discord User ID and join the Lanyard Discord server. Your live game status, custom presence, and voice activity sync instantly via the Lanyard API.

04

Custom Widgets & Sharing

Add custom widgets, art galleries, animated GIFs, and social links. Copy your BioGram profile URL with one click and add it to your Twitter, Instagram, or Discord bio.
